Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

Quote Widget ("the app") is a free, offline-first mobile application that displays personal quotes and reminders on your Android Home Screen. This privacy policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Data Stored on Your Device

All content you create — collections, quotes, widget configurations — is stored entirely on your device using local databases (Hive) and shared preferences. No account is required, and no content is uploaded to any server.

Network Usage

The app connects to the internet only for:

  • AdMob advertisements — banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads are served by Google AdMob. Google's ad framework may collect device identifiers, IP address, and usage data for ad delivery and measurement. For details, see Google's Advertising Policy.
  • Error reporting — crash logs are sent to Sentry to help diagnose bugs. These logs may include device model, OS version, and a stack trace. No personal content or identifiable information is included.

No Personal Data Collection

  • No account creation or login
  • No personal identifiers collected by the app itself
  • No cloud sync or backup to external servers
  • No analytics beyond what Google AdMob and Sentry provide
  • AdMob is configured with non-personalized ads (npa=1)

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob — ad delivery. Subject to Google Privacy Policy.
  • Sentry — crash reporting. Subject to Sentry Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. It contains no age-restricted content.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, open an issue on the QuoteWidget GitHub repository.
