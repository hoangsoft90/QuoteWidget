# Proposal: Onboarding

## What
Implement the first-time user experience: a welcome screen that guides users to either start with sample content or add their own, then leads them through adding their first widget. Target: first widget added within 60 seconds.

## Why
The onboarding is the critical activation funnel. If users don't add a widget in the first minute, they're likely to churn. The flow must be simple, fast, and motivating.

## Scope
- Welcome screen with two clear options: "Start with Sample" or "Add Your Own"
- Sample collections with pre-written content (no celebrity quotes)
- Quick widget add flow after content selection
- Progress indicator showing steps to first widget
- Skip option for users who want to explore first

## Non-goals
- Complex tutorial/walkthrough (keep it simple)
- Account creation (app is offline-only)
- Permission requests (app needs no special permissions)

## Success Criteria
- New user sees welcome screen on first launch
- "Start with Sample" creates sample collections immediately
- "Add Your Own" guides to collection creation
- After content selection, user is guided to add widget
- First widget added within 60 seconds of app open
- User can skip onboarding and explore app freely
