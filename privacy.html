Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

Overview

Quote Widget – Your Words is an offline-first application. Your content (collections, items, widget settings) is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to us or to any server we operate — there is no cloud component, no account, and no backend.

Data You Create

All content you create is stored locally on your device using Hive (a local on-device database). We cannot see, access, or recover this data. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, that content is permanently deleted.

Advertising (Google Mobile Ads)

The app shows rewarded video ads as a way to temporarily unlock Pro features. These ads are served by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). When an ad is displayed, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process the following, subject to Google's own policies:

  • Device advertising identifier (e.g. Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and general device information
  • Interaction data with ads (views, clicks)

Google uses this information to serve and measure ads. You can learn more in Google's advertising policies and Google Privacy Policy. You can reset your Android advertising ID at any time under your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Ads are opt-out by purchase: buying the one-time "Remove Ads Forever" in-app purchase disables rewarded ads entirely, after which the Google Mobile Ads SDK is not used and no ad-related data is collected.

Permissions

The app uses the following device capabilities:

  • Home Screen Widget — to display your chosen content on your home screen (data is stored locally on the device).
  • Internet / Network state — required solely by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to download and display ads. It is not used to upload any of your content.
  • Share target — lets you send text from other apps into Quote Widget for local storage; the shared text is only saved on your device.

Children's Privacy

Quote Widget does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13. Ads served are subject to Google's policies regarding ads in apps directed to children.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the revised version will be available at this URL. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact
