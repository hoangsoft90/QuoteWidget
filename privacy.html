Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2, 2026

Overview

Quote Widget – Your Words is a fully offline application. Your data never leaves your device.

Data Collection

We do not collect any data. The app does not:

  • Collect personal information
  • Track your usage or behavior
  • Use analytics or tracking tools
  • Transmit data over the internet

Data Storage

All content you create (collections, items, widget configurations) is stored locally on your device using Hive (a local database). No data is sent to any server or cloud service.

Permissions

The app does not request any device permissions, including:

  • No INTERNET permission
  • No camera, microphone, or location access
  • No contacts or calendar access

The only system feature used is the Android Home Screen Widget, which stores data locally on your device.

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate with any third-party services, SDKs, or advertising networks.

Children's Privacy

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the revised version will be available at this URL. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
